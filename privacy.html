Privacy Policy

Behind the Brand Publisher (TikTok app) · Last updated 19 October 2026

What the app is

Behind the Brand Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Behind the Brand | ورا البراند channel. It uploads and posts the channel's own videos to the channel's own TikTok account. It is not offered to anyone else, and no other TikTok user can sign in to it.

Data we collect

The only data the app keeps is the OAuth access and refresh token that TikTok issues when the owner authorizes the app for our own account. The token is stored as an encrypted secret in our private GitHub repository (GitHub Actions secrets) and is used only to post our videos.

The app does not collect, read or store data about any other TikTok user: no profiles, followers, comments, messages, contacts or analytics of third parties.

How data is used and shared

The token is used only to call TikTok's API to publish our own videos. We do not sell, rent or share it, or any other data, with anyone. We use no advertising or tracking in the app, and this website sets no cookies.

Retention and deletion

The token is kept until the owner revokes the app's access in TikTok's settings or deletes the secret, which removes it immediately.
